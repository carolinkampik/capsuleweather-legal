Privacy Policy

CapsuleWeather · Last updated 7 October 2026

CapsuleWeather digitises your wardrobe and suggests outfits based on the weather. To do that it stores photos of your clothes and a few preferences about how you dress. This policy explains exactly what is collected, where it goes, and how to get rid of it.

Who processes your data for us

CapsuleWeather runs on infrastructure operated by the following providers. Those that handle personal data on our behalf do so under a data processing agreement, and may use it only to deliver their service to us — never for their own purposes.

Two entries are not our processors. Open-Meteo is called by our server rather than by your phone, and receives a coordinate rounded to about ten kilometres with nothing attached to it, so no personal data reaches them. Apple and Google act as independent controllers for app distribution and payment, under their own privacy policies rather than our instructions.

Your photos are not used to train AI models. Neither provider trains on data submitted through its API.

fal.ai fetches the photo, removes the background and holds the result on its content delivery network only until we have downloaded it — we set it to expire after one hour. OpenAI returns the studio shot directly in its reply and keeps no copy; it retains abuse-monitoring logs of API requests for up to 30 days, as it does for every customer, after which they are deleted.

We keep the processed photo in your wardrobe until you delete the item or your account.

Transfers outside the EEA

Some of these providers process data in the United States. Those transfers rely on the European Commission's Standard Contractual Clauses, or on the EU–US Data Privacy Framework where the provider is certified.

How long we keep it

  • Your account and wardrobe — for as long as your account exists.
  • Deleted items — removed from the wardrobe immediately; purged from storage shortly after.
  • Your whole account — deleted on request. Everything linked to it, including photos and outfit history, is removed.
  • Weather forecast data — cached briefly by rounded coordinate and not linked to your account.
  • Mailing list — until you unsubscribe or delete your account, whichever comes first.
  • Analytics events — retained in aggregate for product analysis.

Your rights

Under the GDPR you may, at any time:

  • ask what we hold about you, and receive a copy
  • have inaccurate data corrected
  • have your data deleted
  • receive your data in a portable, machine-readable format
  • object to processing based on legitimate interest
  • withdraw consent — for location, notifications, marketing emails, or your name — without affecting processing that already happened

Email info@capsuleweather.com and we will answer within 30 days. You can revoke location and notification permission at any time in your device settings, and unsubscribe from marketing emails using the link at the bottom of any of them. You also have the right to complain to your local data protection authority.

Deleting your account

You can delete your account from inside the app: Profile → Privacy & data → Delete account. Your profile, wardrobe photos, outfits and wear history are permanently deleted, and if you were on the mailing list your contact is removed from our email provider in the same step. There is no deactivation step and nothing is kept in a recoverable state.

If you no longer have the app installed, email info@capsuleweather.com from the address your account uses and ask for deletion. We will confirm once it is done.

A subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play must be cancelled separately in your store account — we cannot cancel it for you.

Security

All traffic between the app and our servers is encrypted in transit. Data is encrypted at rest by our hosting provider. Access to your wardrobe is enforced at the database level, so one account cannot read another's data. Sign-in uses a one-time code sent to your email; there is no password to leak.

Children

CapsuleWeather is not directed at children and is intended for users aged 18 and over. We do not knowingly collect data from children. If you believe a child has given us personal data, email us and we will delete it.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes in a way that affects you, we will update the date at the top and, for significant changes, tell you in the app before the change takes effect.
