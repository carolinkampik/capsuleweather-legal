Delete your account

CapsuleWeather · Last updated 6 October 2026

You can delete your CapsuleWeather account and everything in it at any time. You do not need to contact us first, and you do not need a reason.

In the app

Profile → Privacy & data → Delete account

You will be asked to confirm twice. After the second confirmation the deletion happens immediately and you are signed out.

Without the app

If you have already uninstalled CapsuleWeather, email info@capsuleweather.com from the address your account uses and ask us to delete it. We will delete the account and confirm by reply, within 30 days at the latest and usually within a few days.

What is deleted

Everything tied to your account:

  • your account and sign-in details
  • your name and email address
  • every photo of every item in your wardrobe
  • all wardrobe items and their tags
  • all saved outfits
  • your full wear history
  • your dressing preferences, climate zone and temperature thresholds
  • your subscription record

Deletion is permanent. There is no deactivated or frozen state, nothing is archived, and none of it can be restored afterwards — including by us.

What we cannot delete for you

A subscription bought through the App Store or Google Play. That contract is between you and the store, and only you can cancel it from your store account. Deleting your CapsuleWeather account does not cancel it and does not stop it renewing.

  • Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions
  • Google: Play Store → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions
