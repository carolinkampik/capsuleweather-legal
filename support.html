Support

CapsuleWeather · Last updated 17 October 2026

Email info@capsuleweather.com. We answer within a few days, usually sooner. Tell us your device and what you were trying to do.

Signing in

There is no password. Enter your email and we send an 8-digit code. If it does not arrive within a minute, check your spam folder, then tap Resend code.

Subscriptions

CapsuleWeather Premium is billed by the App Store or Google Play, so they manage it:

  • Apple: Settings → your name → Subscriptions
  • Google: Play Store → Payments and subscriptions → Subscriptions

Cancel there at least 24 hours before the current period ends to stop the next renewal. Refunds are handled by the store: for Apple, at reportaproblem.apple.com.

New phone, or Premium missing after reinstalling? Open the paywall and tap Restore purchase, signed in with the same store account.

Your data

  • Privacy policy
  • Delete your account — in the app under Profile → Privacy & data

Deleting your account does not cancel a store subscription. Cancel it in your store account as well.
